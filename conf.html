Conference guidelines

Guidelines for Marcin Moskała presentations and workshops at conferences.

I am open to invitations to conferences, and I am thrilled to share knowledge during Kotlin presentations and workshops, although I have some requirements. When you invite me as a speaker for a conference or a meetup, I expect travel expenses to be covered, including:

  • Flight
  • Hotel with a breakfast

If you invite me, I assume the travel expenses will be covered.

I might make an exception only when:

  • I will appear as a keynote speaker in front of more than 300 people.
  • I will appear on a discussion panel.

Please do not place my presentation before 10 am or as the last presentation of the day, unless it is the opening or closing keynote.

I am open to giving more than one presentation or workshop. I actually prefer an option with both a presentation and a workshop, or two presentations. It makes the time spent on travel more rewarding.
