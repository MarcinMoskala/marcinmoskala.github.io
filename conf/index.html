For conference organizers

I'm happy to speak at conferences and meetups. I prefer to combine a talk with a workshop, or to give two talks, so the trip is worth it for both sides.

What I ask of organizers

  • Travel costs covered: flight, and a hotel with breakfast.
  • Exceptions: keynotes for audiences of 300+ people, and panel discussions.
  • Please don't schedule my session before 10:00 or as the last slot of the day, unless it is an opening or closing keynote.
