Marcin Moskała

Developer, trainer, and author of books on Kotlin.

Since 2017 I have been running kt.academy, teaching Kotlin to developer teams around the world. My books grew out of those workshops; the best known are Effective Kotlin and Kotlin Coroutines: Deep Dive.

I speak at conferences such as KotlinConf and droidcon, make small games for practising code reading, and through Podaj Dalej Programowanie I give away books to people who want to start programming.
