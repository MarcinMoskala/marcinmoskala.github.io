Marcin Moskała

Software architect, teacher and author of popular books.

I am a practising programmer, currently a software architect at Bank Pekao. Building real systems is what everything else I do grows out of.

I write books. Developers around the world learn from Effective Kotlin and Kotlin Coroutines: Deep Dive, my books on Kotlin, the main language for building Android apps. For complete beginners, I wrote JavaScript od podstaw and Python od podstaw in Polish. I also write beyond programming, about critical thinking and stoicism.

I teach. I run workshops for developer teams at companies around the world and have spoken over 100 times, at conferences such as KotlinConf and droidcon. That work grew into kt.academy, the training company I founded, today an official training partner of JetBrains, the creators of Kotlin.

I believe knowledge should be passed on. Through Podaj Dalej Programowanie I have given away over 3,000 books to people who want to start programming, and I make free games and cheat sheets for developers.

Looking for a workshop for your team? See kt.academy. Want to invite me to a conference or a podcast? Message me on LinkedIn.
