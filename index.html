Marcin Moskała

I teach Kotlin and write books about it.

I founded kt.academy, an official JetBrains training partner, and since 2017 I have been teaching Kotlin to developer teams at companies around the world. My books grew out of those workshops; the best known are Effective Kotlin and Kotlin Coroutines: Deep Dive.

I have given over 70 talks, at conferences such as KotlinConf, droidcon, JavaZone and GeeCON. I also build small games that train you to read Kotlin code, and through Podaj Dalej Programowanie I have given away over 3,000 books to people who want to start programming.

Want me to speak at your event, run a workshop for your team, or join your podcast? Write to marcinmoskala@gmail.com.
